## Who she is

- Instagram @adley ~1.4M followers; YouTube @TheAdleyShow ~2.75M subscribers, ~1.6K videos.

## The six-step formula ("billion view formula")

1. **Craft a hook that retains 90% of viewers for 6 seconds.** Use the **combo method**: pair two things that don't usually go together (a rack of ribs on a car engine). Every hook must do two jobs — trigger an emotion and open a curiosity gap.
2. **Storyboard suspenseful storytelling.** The **Missy Elliott method**: "put it down, flip it and reverse it" — design from the opening three seconds backwards. Open on an intriguing scene, never on your end goal. Write the video beat by beat.
3. **Deliver the payoff.** Resolve the curiosity gap in the final 3-10 seconds. A satisfying payoff earns shares and comments; withholding it deliberately pushes viewers to a part two.
4. **Proactively engineer engagement.** Decide the comments you want *before* filming, then build the triggers in: Easter eggs, relatable moments, debate bait, odd wardrobe, intentional misspellings, deliberate gaps that let viewers show expertise.
5. **Raise the stakes.** Four levers: **location** (out of the expected setting), **props** (raise what's at risk), **wardrobe** (signals character with no explanation), **casting** (children, a couple in their seventies — people whose presence changes the emotional weight). "Stakes determine why people care."
6. **Split test.** Make 3-4 versions with different hooks, opening shots, lengths and captions. Load 5-12 versions onto Instagram trial reels, run 24-72 hours, publish the winner. Paid variant: $5-6 per version, ~1,000 views is enough to read a retention curve.

## Rules and numbers

- "The first three seconds matter more than the next 30 ever will."
- Target 90% retention through the first 6 seconds; 82% is still good. ~75% through the first 12 seconds is her strong-potential benchmark.
- A retention drop of 40% or more marks the beat to cut or rewrite.
- Check the script every three seconds: would they keep watching, and why would anyone care?
- **Open loop:** "there is a subconscious itch there that I created that I'm not going to scratch." Give value continuously, hold the payoff to the end.
- **One emotional tone per video.** Pick the target emotion first (learning, humour, fear, trust); switching tone mid-video confuses viewers. Music and text overlays set that tone — the same footage reads differently under suspenseful music.
- **Reps, not time:** "This isn't a time thing. It's definitely a reps thing."
- **Virality is subjective:** her company's bar is 5M views; a channel doing 300-400 views might call 40K viral.
- **Contextual vs cultural relevance:** contextual = known for a service inside a niche; cultural = broad shareable appeal, which makes you contextually relevant by default.
- Funnel split: aim 60%+ top-of-funnel awareness content (she also describes a 50/50 split elsewhere); shares are the weighted signal that finds lookalike audiences.
- Her stated hit rate: roughly 7 in 10 of her videos pass a million views. She explicitly denies every video goes viral.
- Diagnose your own bottleneck first — ideas, time or editing — and fix that one.
- Claims a trial-reel run of 100M+ views and ~50K followers in 30 days.
