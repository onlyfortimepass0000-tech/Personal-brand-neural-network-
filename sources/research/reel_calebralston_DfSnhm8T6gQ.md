## Transcript (condensed)
1. Hook: "How to build a diabolically good personal brand in less than 90 seconds?"
2. Definition: personal branding is an intentional pairing of relevant things done consistently; brand is the byproduct, when the audience associates you with what you paired yourself with.
3. Optimise for trust, not views or virality: customers pay the bills, not views.
4. Find your contrarian takes: what people say about your world that you fundamentally disagree with.
5. Find your pond: the body of water you can compete in and ideally dominate, set by what you have accomplished. He talks about personal brand only for business owners.
6. "Before you do any of the first four": figure out the desired outcome with the Brand Journey framework. Four questions: goal for the brand; what you need to be known for; what you need to do to be known; what you need to learn to do that.
7. Close: "And with all of that, now you know." No call to action.

## On-screen text
Title card with the 90-second promise. Captions in 2-4 word bursts. Cards: "Stop Overcomplicating It", "#2 Optimize For Trust, Not Views", "#3 Find Your Contrarian Take", "#4 Find Your Pond", "#5 Figure Out What Your Goal Is", then a four-question card.

## Storytelling read
- Not a story. A numbered framework list, no scene, person, event or feeling. Principle p6 (specifics and feeling) is almost absent.
- Hook (p3): a question plus a value promise plus a time box. No emotion or gap in the Adley sense; it works because the topic already has demand.
- Open loops (p1, p5): numbered cards promise a count, so viewers wait for the next one. Item 5 is a priority reversal ("before any of the first four"), the one real turn.
- Named ideas (p7): "pond", "Brand Journey framework", the one-line brand definition. He packs five ideas into 84 seconds, which fights his own card "Stop Overcomplicating It".
- Belief (p8): "trust, not views" is his contrarian take used as the reel's own point of view.
- Tension: the reel preaches trust over views, and it landed at about 3x his baseline views. Retention-first creators (Adley) would say the format earned views, not trust.
- Delivery (p9): direct to camera, continuous, caption chunking sets rhythm.
- World (p10): stays inside his business-owner world; 48 comments on about 97K views suggests nothing invited a reply.
