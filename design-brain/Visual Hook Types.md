# Visual Hook Types
Part of [[01 Hook Visuals]].

## Rules
- Five proven first-frame types: motion already happening, object out of place, finished result first, face mid-action, worst before-state.
- Frame one must be a real image, never a fade from black or the first frame of a transition.
- One subject that fills a large part of the frame and separates from the background in brightness.
- Test at feed size: shrink the frame to a third of phone width, look for 1 second, write what it promises. No clear sentence = redo.
