# Hide the Cut
Part of [[03 Motion and Transitions]].

## Rules
- Most seamless transitions cut at peak velocity, where motion blur hides the join.
- Whip pan: 8-12 frames, directional blur matched to direction, whoosh peak on the cut frame.
- In 9:16, vertical whips and fast zooms read better than lateral moves.
