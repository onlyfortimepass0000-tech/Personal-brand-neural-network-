# Daily drop spec (rebuild format, from Nov 28 2026)

Aryan's words (Nov 28, 00:44 IST): "I want to see edits and motion graphics and the way of presenting in terms of creative, which is done by the biggest creators on planet Earth. I want you to extract that and make it... If I like it, I will tell you to implement in maybe the edits of my reels also... if you will include shit things, I have to destroy you."

## What a drop is
At least 4 motion-graphics or editing moments, each taken from a real reel or short by a top creator, traced frame by frame and rebuilt as a working 9:16 animation. No tips from posts, no articles, no generic "trends". Every piece must point to a real reel and a timestamp.

## Where moments come from
- Creators with heavy, original motion graphics first: Kallaway, Alex Hormozi, Dhruv Rathee, plus any explainer editor the run finds whose reels have 1M+ views. Rotate creators and never repeat a moment already in watched/ or a previous rebuild page.
- Instagram: SCRAPECREATORS_LIST_INSTAGRAM_USER_POSTS (1 credit per page, 12 posts). Pick reels with the most plays. Cap stays 4 credits a day.

## How a moment is taken apart
1. Download the reel in the Composio workbench and pull frames at 2 fps into contact sheets (8 x 5 frames, timestamps on each).
2. View the sheets in the cloud browser, mark the moments where a graphic, type move, composite or transition does the work.
3. Pull full-size frames at those timestamps and read the exact layout, colours, type pairing, order of events and timing.
4. Write the beat list: timestamp, what appears, how it moves.

## How it is rebuilt
- A canvas animation in a 9:16 frame that copies the layout, order, timing, type pairing and colours of the original. A stand-in figure takes the creator's place.
- Each card: source (creator, reel title with link, plays, timestamp range), beat-by-beat list, one line on why it works, one line on how it would look in Aryan's reel (Atomz / local-business story).

## Quality bar
- If it can't be traced to a real frame, it doesn't go in.
- If the rebuild doesn't look like the original move at a glance, rework it or drop it.
- Fewer, stronger pieces beat filler. Four is the floor.

## Output
- Publish to the rebuilds page (see artifacts.md). Post to Aryan starting "Your daily design drop:" with one line per piece and the page link, plus credits spent.
- When Aryan picks pieces, those get built into his real reel edits (Remotion in his repo) on his word.
