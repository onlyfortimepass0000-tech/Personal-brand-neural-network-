# Mix Levels
Part of [[08 Sound-Visual Sync]].

## Rules
- Voice about -16 LUFS, music under voice ducked 10-14 dB, master -14 LUFS, true peak -1 dBTP for Reels.
- Talking-head density cap: about 2 SFX per 2 seconds. Repeated hits step down 1.5 dB each.
- Phone speakers drop under 300 Hz: layer a mid transient on bass hits.
