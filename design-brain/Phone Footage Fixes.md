# Phone Footage Fixes
Part of [[05 Colour Light and Texture]].

## Rules
- Phones over-sharpen and over-saturate: lower saturation and contrast slightly, add fine grain.
- Shoot 24-25 fps with shutter near 1/50 for natural motion blur.
- Lock white balance; never mix warm bulbs and daylight on a face.
