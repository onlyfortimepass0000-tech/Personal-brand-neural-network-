# Title Text Hook
Part of [[01 Hook Visuals]].

## Rules
- Title text is not captions: bigger text that says what the video is about, on screen in the first 3 seconds (Kallaway: people read it 10 times out of 10).
- Text on a solid background block. Place it in the 280-450 px band from the top of a 1080x1920 frame.
- Words that match the spoken hook but are not identical; lead with the most charged word.
