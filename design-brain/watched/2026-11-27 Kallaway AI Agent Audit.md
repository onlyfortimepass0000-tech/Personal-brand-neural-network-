## Takeaways for Aryan's reels
1. Hook density: aim for a visual change roughly every 0.8-1 s in the first 10 s, then about every 1.3-1.5 s.
2. Two caption registers: small coloured captions in split screen, one giant white word on full-screen face beats.
3. Put screen recordings in a dark rounded card with a coloured glow rim instead of raw full-screen.
