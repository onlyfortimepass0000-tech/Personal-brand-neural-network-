## Patterns from this batch
1. **Two speeds both win.** Explainers with B-roll run ~50 changes a minute (Kallaway). Authority talking-heads run ~17 (Hormozi). The middle is where reels die.
2. **Motion without cuts.** Caleb and Sam hold one shot and let graphics build over it. A diagram building in place counts as a "change" for the viewer even with no cut.
3. **The face is the anchor.** Kallaway never cuts away from his face fully; the B-roll lives in the top half.
4. **Repurpose, don't redesign.** Hormozi's carousels are tweets and podcast stills. Low design cost, high saves.
