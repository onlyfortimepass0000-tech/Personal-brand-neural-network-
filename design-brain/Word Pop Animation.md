# Word Pop Animation
Part of [[02 Typography and Captions]].

## Rules
- Each word scales in from 60-80% with a fast overshoot, about 200 ms, on its own start time.
- Spring damping about 12, mass 0.6 in Remotion gives the alive overshoot.
