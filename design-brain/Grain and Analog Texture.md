# Grain and Analog Texture
Part of [[05 Colour Light and Texture]].

## Rules
- Film grain 2-5% opacity, paper, VHS, risograph textures signal a human made it (the anti-AI look, 2026 trend).
- Grain should be barely visible paused but alive in motion; too much turns to compression mush.
