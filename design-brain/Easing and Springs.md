# Easing and Springs
Part of [[03 Motion and Transitions]].

## Rules
- Never linear. Ease-out for entrances, ease-in for exits, ease-in-out for moves within the frame.
- Back curves and springs add overshoot = mass and personality; keep it small.
- Align the peaks of position, scale and blur on the same frame.
