# Dead Air Removal
Part of [[04 Pacing and Rhythm]].

## Rules
- Cut gaps over about 0.4 s mid-sentence, breaths, repeats, greetings. A 64 s clip often becomes 48 s with no lines lost.
- Try to cut another 20% after the first edit.
