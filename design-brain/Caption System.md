# Caption System
Part of [[02 Typography and Captions]].

## Rules
- 2 to 4 words per page, max 2 lines, centered just below the middle (about 62-70% down).
- Heavy sans in caps: Montserrat Black 900, Anton, TheBoldFont, Rocca One Heavy (Kallaway).
- Size about 56-92 px on 1080x1920; 6-9 px black stroke or a soft shadow so it reads on any background.
- Word-level timing from Whisper; never split a line evenly over time.
