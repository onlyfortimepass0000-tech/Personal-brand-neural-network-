# Visual Switching
Part of [[04 Pacing and Rhythm]].

## Rules
- Change something on screen every 2-4 seconds: angle, punch-in, b-roll, text, graphic.
- Kallaway's stun gun: switch visuals fast enough to keep the subconscious busy, not overwhelmed. Jump cuts count.
- Cut when the idea changes, not on a stopwatch (shots then vary 1.5-6 s).
