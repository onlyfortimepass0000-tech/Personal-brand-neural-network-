# Power Word Highlight
Part of [[02 Typography and Captions]].

## Rules
- One highlighted word per phrase, in one accent colour (yellow, green or brand). Two highlights become noise.
- Vary register: switch from coloured captions to plain white bold for quotes or emphasis so the eye does not go stale (Kallaway).
