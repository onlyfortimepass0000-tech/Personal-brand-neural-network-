# SFX on Motion
Part of [[08 Sound-Visual Sync]].

## Rules
- Whoosh on camera moves and whips with its loudest pass on the cut frame; swoosh on title entrances; impact on landings.
- Riser into a reveal, then a 2-3 keyframe huge jump on the hit (Kallaway).
