# Own reels scoreboard

Source: Windsor.ai for Social Media, Instagram connector, account @atomz_growthagency (Aryan plans to rename it as his personal brand, Nov 27 2026). Free, no credits.

## How the daily run scores a reel
Pull once a reel is 48 h old: `media_reel_skip_rate` (share who swipe in the first 3 s), `media_reel_avg_watch_time` (ms), video length, `media_reach`, `media_views`, `media_saved`, `media_shares`.
- **Hook score** = 1 - skip rate. Target: skip rate under 50%.
- **Hold** = avg watch time / length. Target: over 50%.
- **Spread** = (saves + shares) / reach.
Tag each reel with the brain techniques it used (split screen, word slam, prop metaphor, graphics build-in-place, speed band ~17 or ~50/min). After 8-10 reels, rank techniques by hook score and hold, and lead the daily drop with the winners. Log each reel as a row below.

- Images and the carousel: reach 12-74, almost no saves or shares.
- Read: 4 in 5 viewers left inside 3 seconds. The first frame is the whole problem; see [[01 Hook Visuals]] and Patterns 1, 10, 11.
