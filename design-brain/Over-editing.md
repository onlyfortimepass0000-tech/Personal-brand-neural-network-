# Over-editing
Problem node, linked from [[00 Core]].

- Kallaway (2026): brains are overstimulated; flashbangs, stacked effects and SFX on every cut hurt comprehension. Raw content is rising.
- Rule: give every effect a written reason; if none, delete it. 11 zooms in 30 s became 3 and read better.
- Tension: hooks must cut through, yet the body must stay calm. Resolution: hook strength comes from the base visual, not added layers.
