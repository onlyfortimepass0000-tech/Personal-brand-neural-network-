# Punch-in and Snapback
Part of [[03 Motion and Transitions]].

## Rules
- Alternate original framing and a 10-20% punch-in on every other jump cut so cuts look deliberate.
- Snapback: zoom on the first of two full-screen clips, return the second to normal framing (Kallaway).
- Snap zoom: scale 20-40% over 2-4 frames on a key word.
- Slight push-in on any static full-screen b-roll so it never feels frozen.
