# Transition Grammar by Tone
Part of [[03 Motion and Transitions]].

## Rules
- Use three transition grammars only: lateral slide, vertical rise, settle (Remocn Opus 5 notes).
- Big tonal jumps (dark to paper) take the settle, so the colour change happens in an empty gap frame.
- No scene arrives blank: pre-roll the incoming scene 12-20 frames so its first element is already moving.
