# Composition Rules
Part of [[06 Composition and Framing]].

## Rules
- Eyes on the top third line; chest-up; small headroom.
- Hands, product and screen in the middle third. Move toward or away from camera, not across.
- Lens at eye level, 60 cm to 1 m away (phone lenses distort close).
