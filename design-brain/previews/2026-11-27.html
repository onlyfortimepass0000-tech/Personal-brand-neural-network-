Title text hook Hook visuals

Big text on a solid block in the first 3 seconds, separate from captions. Kallaway says viewers read it 10 times out of 10, and it has to match what you say and what you show.

In your edit: Anton, off-white block with the charged word on yellow, top band about 280 to 450 px down a 1080×1920 frame. Slide in over 8 frames with ease-out.

Punch-in snapback Motion

On the key word, scale the face clip up fast, then snap the next clip back to normal framing. It makes cuts look deliberate without adding layers.

In your edit: scale 100 to 115% over 3 frames on the word, hard cut to the next clip at 100%. Remotion: interpolate(frame,[f,f+3],[1,1.15]).

Power-word captions Typography

Three words a page, heavy caps, thick black stroke, and one word in the accent. Two highlighted words in one line turn into noise.

In your edit: Montserrat 900 at about 80 px, 6 to 9 px stroke, each word pops from 60% with a spring (damping 12, mass 0.6) on its own Whisper timestamp.

Number that lands Graphics + sound

A count-up stat card turns a claim into proof. Only show numbers you actually say, and put a soft impact sound on the frame it stops.

In your edit: count over about 0.8 s with ease-out, card on a dark translucent panel, yellow digits. Impact peak around −7 dBFS, once per reel.
