Launch-film craft, cut for a 9:16 feed

Five motion pieces built the way OpenAI, Anthropic and Studio Dumbar build theirs, using your own lines and numbers. Each one is a single function of time, so it renders frame-exact in Remotion and drops over your A-roll as an overlay.

01 / THE POINT

Dot to proof

Stolen from OpenAI "Refreshed"

One dot types the hook with a tick per letter, deletes it, swells, bursts into rings, then the particles assemble your number.

Why it stops a thumb: the eye can't predict what the dot becomes, so it waits to find out.

Build it: letters every 3 frames, burst on a downbeat, 1 s morph staggered by position. Particles only for the reveal, never as wallpaper.

02 / DECODE

Terminal decode hook

Stolen from Anthropic's Claude Code launch

Heavy type scrambles through glyphs and locks left to right, then each line exits by rising behind its own mask.

Why it stops a thumb: scrambled letters read as "something is loading" and pull the eye for the payoff word.

Build it: glyph swap every 45 ms, each letter locks 45 ms after the last, last word in the accent. Under 4 words, or it becomes noise.

03 / GRAVITY

Your belief, dropped

Stolen from physics type, Memorables 2026

Your line "Discipline without direction is just a better 9-to-5" falls word by word, squashes on landing, and the last word hits with a shake.

Why it stops a thumb: weight is a feeling. Each landing is a beat the viewer feels before reading.

Build it: words fall on t² for 0.42 s, squash 15% on impact, rotation settles on a spring. Shake 3 frames, only on the final word.

04 / EVERY DOT

300 calls, counted

Stolen from the Remocn Opus 5 film notes

An odometer rolls to 300+, then a grid of 300 dots fills in a wave. Each dot is one of your real cold calls.

Why it stops a thumb: a unit chart turns a number into effort you can see. The paper stage also breaks the dark-feed pattern.

Build it: opacity on a short ramp, movement on a spring 3× longer. Stagger by pow(i, 0.8). Spend the accent only on the payoff.

05 / GLASS

Liquid glass caption card

Stolen from Apple Liquid Glass and editorial type

A shader light field moves behind a frosted panel. The caption mixes an italic serif with heavy caps and blurs into focus word by word.

Why it stops a thumb: it looks like nothing else in a feed full of yellow karaoke captions.

Build it: blur the background inside the panel at about 18 px, 1 px white rim at 25%, words sharpen from 14 px blur over 0.35 s.
