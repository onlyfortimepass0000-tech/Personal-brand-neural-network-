# Pause Grammar

Source: Reddit r/VideoEditing quotes and Sycamore retention study (8 Oct 2026)

A pause before a line works, a pause after is dead. Gaps 0.15-0.25 s; keep 0.3-0.6 s before a number or reveal; punch in 115-120% on every 2nd-3rd cut; J-cut so removed pauses aren't visual jumps; tight talking head 180-200 wpm. Jenny Hoyos cut 1 s from a Short: retention ~83% to ~88%. Direct fix for Aryan's 81% skip.
