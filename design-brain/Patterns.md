# Patterns (interconnections found across balls)
1. **The eye trusts what the ear confirms.** A visual change lands harder with a sound on the same frame: [[Hide the Cut]] + [[SFX on Motion]], [[Numbers That Land]] + [[Beat Grid]], [[Word Pop Animation]] on the word's own start.
2. **One accent, everywhere.** The same single accent colour drives [[Power Word Highlight]], [[Callouts and Arrows]] and [[Accent Colour Rule]]. Narrow accent = sharper attention.
3. **Difference beats loudness.** [[Contrast and Saliency]] (be the singleton), [[Pacing Tiers]] (fast then slow) and [[Power Word Highlight]] (one word, not all) are the same law: emphasis comes from contrast, not volume.
4. **Alignment is the hook.** [[Visual Hook Types]], [[Title Text Hook]] and the spoken line must match ([[Hook Alignment]]); the same alignment rule runs through the whole body ([[Key Visual]]).
5. **Safe zones shape everything.** [[Safe Zones]] decide where captions, title text, faces and graphics can live, so they sit upstream of type, composition and overlays.
6. **Depth is the premium cue.** [[Depth Layers]], [[Text Behind Subject]], [[Masking]] and [[Particles and 3D]] all fake a 3D world in a flat phone frame.
7. **Human texture beats polish in 2026.** [[Grain and Analog Texture]], natural delivery and restraint ([[Over-editing]]) all point the same way.
8. **Big-studio craft is restraint plus one moment.** OpenAI, Anthropic and Studio Dumbar each spend motion on one orchestrated moment and keep the rest still: [[Construction Before Result]], [[Decode Type]], [[Transition Grammar by Tone]], [[One Sound Per Event]].
9. **Measured, not assumed.** First watched reel (Kallaway, 929K): median 1.32 s between visual changes, hook twice as dense as the body. See watched/2026-11-27 Kallaway AI Agent Audit.
