# Carousels (posts)

Linked from [[00 Core]], [[02 Typography and Captions]], [[06 Composition and Framing]], [[04 Pacing and Rhythm]].

## Measured
- Top Carousels library, 7,394 Instagram carousels scored against each creator's own usual (read Nov 27 2026): how often a format hit 2x the creator's usual.
  - Reference / cheat sheet: 40% (small sample, 89 posts)
  - How-to: 27%
  - List: 26%
  - Opinion: 24%
  - Story and case study: 22% each
- Best length: 8-12 slides. Best first slide: 6-10 words. Shorter carousels and longer hooks both did worse.
- Instagram re-serves a skipped carousel starting on slide 2 (Mosseri, via Tweenery), so slide 2 is a second hook.

## The build rules
1. **Slide 1 = a reel's first frame.** One claim, big type, a number or a named outcome, plus a swipe cue (arrow or an element running off the edge). It is also the 3:4 grid tile, so keep the focal point inside the crop.
2. **Slide 2 stands alone.** Write it as a hook for people who skipped slide 1, never "here's what happened next".
3. **One idea per slide.** Headline up to ~8 words, body up to ~30. On a 1080 px slide, body below ~40 px is too small on a phone.
4. **One type system.** Same headline font, size range and position on every slide. Fixed margin (~80 px), slide number in the same corner.
5. **Re-hook mid-carousel.** A pattern break slide: different background, one huge number or a bold quote. Same job as a punch-in in a reel ([[Punch-in and Snapback]]).
6. **Payoff slide is the screenshot.** The checklist or framework, not "follow for more".
7. **Ratio:** 3:4 (1080x1440) so slide 1 isn't cropped on the grid.

## Bridge to reels
The same brain applies: hook density up front, one idea per beat, object metaphors ([[Unit Chart]], Patterns 10) beat decoration. Aryan's house accent [[Aryan House Style]] carries over as the one accent colour.
