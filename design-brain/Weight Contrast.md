# Weight Contrast
Part of [[02 Typography and Captions]].

## Rules
- Pair a heavy weight (700-900) for the hero noun or number with a 400-500 weight for connecting words.
- Custom brand type makes you recognisable in the feed before the handle is read; stock karaoke captions read as a template.
