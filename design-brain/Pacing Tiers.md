# Pacing Tiers
Part of [[04 Pacing and Rhythm]].

## Rules
- Hook 0.5-2 s per shot; montage 0.5-1.5 s; explanation 2-4 s; payoff 2-5 s, often slow-mo; ending 1-2 s.
- Contrast makes emphasis: a fast run followed by a slow reveal makes the reveal land.
