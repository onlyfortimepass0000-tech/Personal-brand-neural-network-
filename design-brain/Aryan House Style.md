# Aryan House Style (starting proposal; change freely)
- **Type:** Anton for title text and hero words; Montserrat 800 for captions, 3 words a page, one power word in the accent. See [[Weight Contrast]].
- **Colours:** near-black #0B0B0F, off-white #F5F2EA, one accent: warm signal yellow #FFC83D (caption power word, arrows, stat numbers). See [[Accent Colour Rule]].
- **Grade:** warm cinematic at about 50%, grain 3%. See [[Grade Order]].
- **Layout:** title text block in the top band in the first 3 s; captions just below centre; alternate full and half screen. See [[Safe Zones]], [[Split and Half Screen]].
- **Motion:** punch-in on every other cut, snapback between two face clips, springs not linear. See [[Punch-in and Snapback]].
- **Sound:** whoosh on moves, one impact per reel on the payoff, master -14 LUFS. See [[Mix Levels]].
