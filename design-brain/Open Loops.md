# Open Loops
Part of [[04 Pacing and Rhythm]].

## Rules
- Raise a question in the first seconds, answer it near the end. Reveal a payoff preview up front.
- End on the payoff or the reaction; no end card. Clean end into a strong first frame encourages loops.
