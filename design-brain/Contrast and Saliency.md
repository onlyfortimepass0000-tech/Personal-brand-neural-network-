# Contrast and Saliency
Part of [[05 Colour Light and Texture]].

## Rules
- Pop-out comes from difference: be the only warm frame among cool ones (Treisman feature singleton).
- Luminance does the work: if the subject still separates in greyscale, the frame is sound.
- Text over video: aim above 4.5:1 contrast.
