# Accent Colour Rule
Part of [[05 Colour Light and Texture]].

## Rules
- Three functional colours: background, text, one accent. Narrow accent sets sharpen attention (EEG study, J. Neuroscience).
- Use the brightest colour only for the one thing to look at second.
