# Decode Type
Part of [[02 Typography and Captions]].

## Rules
- Letters scramble through glyphs and lock left to right (Anthropic Claude Code launch). Swap every 45 ms, lock every 45 ms.
- Keep it under 4 words and pair with a mono label; restraint is the premium signal.
