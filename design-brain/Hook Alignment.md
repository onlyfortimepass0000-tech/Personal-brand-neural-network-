# Hook Alignment
Part of [[01 Hook Visuals]].

## Rules
- The visual, the title text and the spoken line must say the same thing (Kallaway). Misalignment kills the hook.
- The visual should complement the curiosity loop: show the exact thing the line names, with an arrow if needed.
