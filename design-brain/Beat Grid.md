# Beat Grid
Part of [[08 Sound-Visual Sync]].

## Rules
- Cut on downbeats; at 30 fps pick 90/100/120/150 BPM so beats land on whole frames (120 BPM = 0.5 s beat).
- Sound may lead the picture by 1-2 frames but never trail it by more than 2 frames.
