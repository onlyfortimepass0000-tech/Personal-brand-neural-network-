# Result First Structure
Part of [[01 Hook Visuals]].

## Rules
- Move the payoff, number or finished result into the first 2 seconds, then rewind to how.
- Clips whose first sentence stands alone and opens a question beat those that do not (Captik study of 1,431 Hindi/Hinglish/English clips: 48% vs 22% opened a question).
